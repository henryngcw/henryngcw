2. Business models & time-to-profit

Break-even is ~RM675/mo. Every model clears it with a single client — the difference is scalability, not whether you profit.

AVOID

A · Hourly billing

Charge per hour worked.

Profit: never scales

  • AI collapses your hours → you earn less for being fast
  • Income capped by hours in a day
OK START

B · Project / fixed-price

Scoped packages, RM2.5k–25k each.

Profit: week 1 of client #1

  • One RM2,500 site covers ~4 months of cost
  • Feast-or-famine — restart pipeline each project
THE GOAL

C · Retainers (recurring)

Care/growth plans RM300–2,500/mo.

Profit: compounds, ~85% margin

  • Flat costs + recurring revenue = scalable
  • 5 retainers ≈ RM5k/mo, covers cost 7×
  • Slower to build — needs delivered projects first
BEST BLEND

D · Productized + retainer

B funds now, C makes it a business.

Profit: month 1; stable by ~month 4–6

  • Projects pay bills, retainers stack underneath
  • Recommended model

5. Your biggest blocker right now

It's not cost. It's not tech. It's pipeline — getting clients.

Your costs are trivial (RM675) and Claude handles delivery. You are not constrained by money or capacity — you're constrained by demand. One signed client makes you profitable; the whole game is finding them.

To accelerate, ignore everything except this:
  • 1 portfolio proof — ship one real site (even a free/cheap first client) to show, not tell.
  • A pricing sheet — so quoting takes minutes, not days.
  • 3–5 outreach attempts/week — local SMEs, niche down (e.g. "websites for KL dental clinics").
  • Convert every project → retainer on delivery. That's the scalable engine.

Don't pre-buy Max 20x, Vercel Pro, or accounting software. Don't over-build infra. Spend energy on sales, not setup.
